# ADR 0002: Chief KPIs and cost discipline

- Status: Accepted
- Date: Not published
- Decider: Tishan Perera, owner

## Context

The org has an HQ page with office floors per department, a top-down org chart, a people list with filters, and an activity timeline. Dashboard polish can take build time that a business question never asked for. The weekday morning brief includes a usage limit.

## Decision

Each chief has a weekly KPI.

Dashboard polish needs a business KPI and a usage check first.

## How "chief" is read on this chart

The decision uses the word chief. Titles on this chart that include that word are:

- Chief of Staff & COO (Amanda)
- Chief of Mailroom
- Chief of YouTube
- Chief of Investments
- Chief Design Engineer (Lex)

KPI figures are not published in this repo. Seats whose titles use a different word are outside this list. This note records the titles the word already matches.

## Consequences

- Each chief keeps a weekly KPI.
- Dashboard polish waits for a business KPI and a usage check.
- The usage limit is a section of the weekday morning brief.
- This record publishes no KPI value, no usage total, and no cost figure.

The morning brief's usage limit and the usage check in this decision are both about cost. This ADR does not claim they are one number.
